30 days of writing · 12 October – 10 November 2026

Write something every day for 30 days. Any length, any form: a page of a novel, a poem, a journal entry, three sentences you'll delete tomorrow. It all counts.

  • Check in once a day using the form link in the group chat.
  • Your day ends at your own midnight. Missed a check-in? Pick the earlier date on the form.
  • Word counts, links and notes are optional. The tick is the point.
  • Late joiners welcome. Start from whichever day you're on.
